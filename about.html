About & Manifesto

Why GeneratingArt.com exists and what we are exploring together.

For as long as humans have gathered around fires, carved into cave walls, or laid ink to paper, we have made art. It isn’t just a pastime—it’s how we process what it feels like to be alive, capturing what is beautiful, disturbing, abstract, or simple. Even a stick figure carries a spark of human intent and understanding.

Throughout history, our tools have continuously evolved. Charcoal gave way to ground pigments and oil paint; the printing press democratized written thought; photography challenged realism; and digital styluses replaced canvas with pixels. Today, we stand on a new frontier: Artificial Intelligence.

AI is fundamentally different from the tools before it. It isn't just a sharper lens or a brush that dries faster; it is an intelligent system we partner with, guide, and train to bring latent ideas out of our heads and into the world.

Learning in Public

GeneratingArt.com was created not as a lecture from a world-renowned engineer, but as an open learning journal. It is a home to explore, demystify, and figure out this new medium together, from square one.

Just as a classic art studio journal explores the chemistry, medium, and soul of paint, we explore both the why and the how of computational creativity:

  • The Philosophy (The "Why"): Ruminating on the nature of art, its history, meaning, and the enduring importance of human intent.
  • The Field Guides (The "How"): Plain-English breakdowns of systems, workflows, models, and practical tools to achieve genuine artistic ends.

Whether you are a traditional artist curious about digital tools, a writer exploring visual mediums, or a creator navigating the future of technology—you are welcome here. Let's see what we can create together.
